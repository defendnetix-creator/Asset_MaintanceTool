## Classification contract

- **OBSERVED** — directly captured UI, route, form, table, experiment, manifest, or network metadata.
- **INFERRED** — a reasoned conclusion supported by evidence but not a directly observed private implementation.
- **PROPOSED** — a decision candidate for the original product; not approved merely by inclusion here.
- **UNKNOWN** — not established by the evidence.
- **CONFLICTING** — evidence disagrees, uses undocumented counting scopes, or an automation result is unreliable.

This evidence is behavioral research only. It does not grant permission to copy the reference name, logos, visual identity, source code, copyrighted assets, or private implementation. The inferred logical model is never the reference application's physical database, captured endpoints are not normative API contracts, and the incomplete Manager comparison cannot support a complete role matrix.

## Count reconciliation

- **Reconciled:** 1,544 inventoried files and zero parse failures agree across applicable files.
- **Repeated synthesized totals:** 149 routes, 285 screens, 258 forms, 96 relationships, 167 application endpoints, and 158 rules.
- **Different units, not direct conflicts:** 394 total endpoints versus 167 application endpoints; raw state/screenshot/form/table artifact counts versus normalized observations.
- **Unresolved:** 17 entities versus 16 classified/enumerated entities; 15 technologies versus 10 observed technologies/services.
- **Not an objective readiness measure:** 7/10 and approximately 78% have no supplied formula and must not be represented as production equivalence.

2. **UNKNOWN:** Complete role and permission matrix, especially Manager behavior and server-side enforcement per action.
